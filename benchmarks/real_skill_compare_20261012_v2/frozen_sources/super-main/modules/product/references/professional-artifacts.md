# Professional artifact fit: resumes, portfolios and proposals

Use only for professional content/artifacts, including pages or exported documents containing them.
Start with [task framing](../../../core/task-framing.md). Do not turn this resume example into a
universal product template. The user's chosen audience and purpose govern the artifact.

## Content before presentation

Identify the artifact: hiring resume, academic CV, executive biography, project case study,
portfolio or proposal. A screenshot-rich case study is not interchangeable with a hiring resume.
Define the reader's decision and choose information density and length for that context. One or
two pages is a common working default for a concise industry resume, not a universal prohibition
on longer CVs or executive histories. Respect explicit recipient requirements.

For a leadership-targeted resume, inspect evidence of technical strategy, organizational scope,
team leadership, hiring/mentoring, budget ownership, risk and business outcomes where applicable.
For an engineering-targeted resume, inspect technical scope, ownership, constraints and measured
results. Tools and visual choices can support project details but cannot substitute for outcomes.

Preserve supplied titles as historical facts; distinguish held role from desired role. Missing
evidence for a target role warrants a gap report or conditional draft, not an invented promotion
or an unsolicited downgrade. Startup/founder leadership can be real; establish context rather
than discrediting it because the company is small or self-founded.

## Claim inventory

For every material statement record supplied fact, supporting artifact and permitted wording.
Never invent employer names, month/year dates, team sizes, revenue, ROI, uptime or customer counts.
If confidentiality prevents naming clients, use an accurate authorized descriptor and retain
dates, role and engagement context where known. Request only missing facts essential to the draft.

For percentages specify denominator, period, environment and measurement. Distinguish achieved
uptime from an SLO; implemented capability from production usage; contribution from sole ownership.
Do not infer scale from a technology label. SQLite's suitability depends on usage and deployment;
see [official SQLite guidance](https://www.sqlite.org/whentouse.html). This is also why a monolith
alone cannot validate or refute a national-scale claim.

## Output review

Review in order: role/audience fit -> chronology -> claim support -> hierarchy -> final medium.
Separate optional case studies or portfolio links when they overwhelm the resume's hiring purpose.
For a PDF deliverable render and inspect every page at a readable scale: clipping, tiny images,
unexpected blank pages, separated headings, font/RTL errors, selectable text and working links.
Inspect the actual PDF for localhost URLs, browser date/header/footer and page breaks. Browser
headers/footers are controlled by export settings as well as print layout; print CSS alone is not
a reliable guarantee. Re-export and inspect after a correction. If no PDF is produced or renderer
is available, explicitly leave PDF quality unverified rather than treating HTML as proof.

Completion requires a recipient-appropriate artifact with supported claims and a checked final
format. The automated claims trace can help locate code claims; it does not establish employment
history, executive capability or hiring suitability.
